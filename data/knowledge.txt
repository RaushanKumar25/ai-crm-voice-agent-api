================================================================================
FORTIVEX CRM - PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE, PRICING, & SUPPORT POLICIES
================================================================================

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
Fortivex CRM (https://fortivex.com) is an enterprise-grade, AI-native B2B Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform designed specifically for high-velocity sales and customer operations teams. Fortivex transforms voice and video conversations into real-time CRM updates, automates call summaries, scores leads autonomously using large language models, and equips revenue teams with real-time AI voice agents for inbound qualification and outbound demo scheduling.

Headquarters: San Francisco, California
Target Customers: Fast-growing B2B SaaS, mid-market fintech, logistics, and enterprise sales teams.

2. CORE PLATFORM CAPABILITIES
- Autonomous Voice Agent: Inbound call assistant that screens prospective leads, answers product inquiries from verified knowledge chunks, and directly books callbacks and product demos into sales calendars.
- Real-Time Call Transcription & Sentiment Analysis: Transcribes inbound and outbound phone calls with sub-300ms latency, extracts key customer pain points, calculates lead qualification scores (1 to 10), and gauges customer sentiment (Positive, Neutral, Negative).
- Automated CRM Synchronization: Instantly logs summarized calls, sentiment flags, and recommended next steps to leading CRM systems including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, and Microsoft Dynamics 365.
- Telephony & Contact Center Integrations: Native plug-and-play integrations with Twilio Voice, Zoom Phone, Dialpad, Aircall, Genesys Cloud, and Amazon Connect.
- Compliance & Zero-Retention Voice Pipeline: Built with enterprise privacy in mind, offering zero-data audio retention, PII redaction, and encrypted voice token streams.

5. BILLING, TRIAL, & CANCELLATION POLICIES
- Free Trial: Fortivex provides a 14-day fully featured free trial on the Growth tier with 100 complimentary AI voice minutes. No credit card is required to sign up.
- Money-Back Guarantee: All new annual subscriptions include an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied within the first 30 days of an annual plan, you will receive a 100% refund.
- Cancellation: Monthly subscriptions can be canceled at any time from the Fortivex Admin Dashboard. The subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle, and no further charges will apply.
- Data Retention Upon Cancellation: If an account is canceled, account owners have 30 days to export their CRM logs, call transcripts, and analytics. After 30 days, all customer data is permanently and securely purged in accordance with DoD 5220.22-M standards.

6. SECURITY, PRIVACY, & COMPLIANCE
- Certifications: SOC 2 Type II certified annually, ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant.
- HIPAA Compliance: HIPAA-ready with execute-ready Business Associate Agreements (BAA) for healthcare customers on the Enterprise tier.
- Encryption Standards: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. Telephony audio streams are encrypted via Secure RTP (SRTP).
- Model Training Policy: Customer audio, call transcripts, and proprietary CRM data are NEVER used to train base foundation AI models. Data privacy and isolation are strictly enforced.

7. VOICE AGENT BEHAVIOR & CALL HANDLING RULES
- Grounding: The AI voice agent answers questions using verified knowledge base facts only. If a question is outside the scope of documentation, the agent must politely explain that it does not possess that information and offer to schedule a callback with a human specialist.
- Tone: Professional, courteous, crisp, and concise (optimized for spoken audio with 1 to 3 sentences per response).
- Follow-up Booking: When a caller expresses interest in scheduling a demo, callback, or follow-up, the agent promptly gathers the customer's name, preferred date/time, and topic, and executes the scheduling tool.
